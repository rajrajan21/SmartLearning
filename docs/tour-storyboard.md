# SharpStudy tour video: script and storyboard

Length: English 1:23 (with voice), Tamil 1:36 (Tamil captions and music; Tamil voice pending). 1280x720, H.264/AAC, about 4.5 and 5.5 MB.

Everything is made locally and free: scenes are drawn by `tools/tour/stage/stage.html`, real screens come from `tools/tour/shots`, frames are captured with headless Chrome and joined with ffmpeg by `tools/tour/render.py`. The wording is in `tools/tour/script.json` (English and Tamil). Rebuild with `Build-Tour-Video.bat`.

Only features that exist today are shown. Practice and exam papers, the E-Library and General Knowledge appear only on the "coming soon" card.
