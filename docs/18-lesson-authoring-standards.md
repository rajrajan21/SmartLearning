# Lesson authoring standards (apply to EVERY chapter and lesson we build)

Owner instructions, to be followed for all remaining lessons, chapters and subjects (Class 3 first, then 4 to 7).

## 1. Classroom (picture) lessons
- Every lesson gets a big picture classroom: coloured drawings, 3D solids where they help, and animation wherever it can be drawn in code (flowing water from a tap, falling rain, turning solids, growing plants, moving clock hands). No image files and no outside services.
- Use the `flow` object for falling or flowing water and the other animated kinds; add new animated kinds to the board when a picture needs them.
- While a question is showing, the board shows only the pictures. All writing on the board (labels such as "6 faces, 12 edges") is hidden so it cannot give the answer away, and 3D solids keep turning. The question and its options appear only in the left panel.
- Every step has English and Tamil narration; new narration needs audio from Generate-Lesson-Audio.bat.

## 2. Question quality (multiple choice)
- Every wrong option must be relevant to the question: something a child could really mix up. Never use silly or unrelated options (no "a pillow" for "which gives us water").
- Each wrong option has a short, kind `whyWrong` explanation. The correct answer has an explanation.
- Use 3 or 4 options. Wrong options must be clearly wrong but believable (common mistakes first).
- Mix question types: mcq, true or false, number, and short answers.

## 3. Content to build for every lesson
Each lesson page carries these tabs, built from original questions (written by us, aligned to the NCERT chapter; online papers are for ideas only, never copied):
1. **Lesson** (classroom)
2. **Quiz**: 8 to 10 quick multiple-choice questions
3. **Practice**: 12 or more questions in 4 rounds (easy to hard) with hints
4. **Worksheet**: printable, 15 mixed questions, answer key for signed-in parents
5. **Exam practice**: questions tagged with the term they suit

## 4. School exam papers
For each subject, each chapter and each lesson we build, add question papers for the three terms:
- Quarterly (first term test), Half-yearly, Annual.
- Papers use the usual school pattern: objective (multiple choice, fill in the blank, true or false), short answer, and long answer, with marks and a time limit, plus a marking scheme.
- Papers are original practice papers modelled on the school pattern. Do not copy a school's or publisher's paper.

## 5. Before a lesson is called done
Check list: classroom steps animated, no board writing during questions, relevant options, quiz + practice + worksheet + term papers present, Tamil text present, audio generated, tests pass.
